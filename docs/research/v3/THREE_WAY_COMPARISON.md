Target: WiredDisplay (`de29a51`) vs SideScreen (`6ec6e317`) vs OpenDisplay (`7f61aeb6`).

| ID | Subsystem | WiredDisplay (Current) | SideScreen (MIT) | OpenDisplay (GPL-3.0) | Classification | Selected Decision & Justification |
