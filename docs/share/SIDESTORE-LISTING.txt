WiredDisplay: SideStore Connect listing (English, the app's primary language)

APP NAME
WiredDisplay

SUBTITLE (short)
Your iPad as a wired display for your Mac

DESCRIPTION
Turn your iPad into a second display for your Mac over the USB-C cable, with sound, touch, keyboard and trackpad.

WiredDisplay works like Sidecar, but only over the cable. The Mac creates a real virtual display at your iPad's native resolution and streams it to the iPad with low latency. It can even be the only screen of a headless Mac mini.

This app is the iPad side. You also need the free WiredDisplayHost app on the Mac (macOS 14 or later), from github.com/sibawono/WiredDisplay/releases.

Features
- Wired only: video, audio and input travel over the USB-C cable. No Wi-Fi, and the Mac opens no network port.
- Sharp picture: HEVC at 60 Hz (up to 120 Hz on iPad Pro), with Balanced, Pro (10-bit), Ultra (4:4:4, 10-bit) and High Color presets.
- More space: Default, More Space and Most Space sizes, remembered for each iPad.
- Touch like Sidecar: tap to click, drag to scroll, pinch to zoom, two-finger tap to right-click, three-finger copy, paste, undo and redo.
- Magic Keyboard and trackpad: full keyboard, smooth scrolling, real pinch and rotate gestures, and Mac shortcuts such as Control-Tab for Command-Tab.
- Sound both ways: Mac audio on the iPad speakers, and the iPad microphone on the Mac.
- Follows the Mac: display sleep, system sleep and the lock screen are handled safely. The iPad never shows the desktop while the Mac is locked.
- A Controls button you can drag to the left or right edge, at any height. It fades while you work.

Requirements
- iPad with iPadOS 17 or later
- Mac with macOS 14 or later (Apple Silicon recommended)
- A USB-C data cable
- Input from the iPad is off until you turn it on in the Mac's menu bar panel.

Open source: github.com/sibawono/WiredDisplay
